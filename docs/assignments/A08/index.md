## CAD Desing:
* The CAD for this assignment was a lot harder than I thought it was going to be. It took me multiply tries to reach almost exactly what I wanted. Even from there it could still use a lot of revisions. Learning how to mate parts and getting gears rotating together was also hard. I did not put a small motor on my gear for this assignment because it was not needed. But to see the efficacy of my box I made you can download the cad file and rotate them by mouse.
